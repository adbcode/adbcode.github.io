In recent Dev Day, OpenAI made tons of announcements, some that caught more attention than others. The one I presonally found interesting was the [Sign In With ChatGPT](https://developers.openai.com/siwc) feature.

Today, most of the "AI apps" sitting on top of some inference provider works by creating a product on top while serving the user through their own API plan. While costing way more than an average user pays for their subscription (if at all) for similar usage. Unless the moat is to work across different providers, having this option can be quite handy for both AI apps (with their margin) and its users (reduced ways of spending on inference).

I tested out the [cookbook](https://github.com/openai/sign-in-with-chatgpt-devkit) for checking this out. It was only appropriate to revisit my [recipe-rag](https://github.com/adbcode/recipe-rag) and to rethink it in this approach. The main challenge with recreating the magic was restoring the original dataset (no longer downloadable, while the publisher's website still exists), and how to avoid using a vector database. Regardless, whether it is the cookbook example or my own work, it was quite simple to get it working. The app developer also has a decent visibility into the different models available to them, while some restrictions exist in the types of requests possible today.

Beyond the commercial side of things (I recently checked out Devin post-Windsurf and it had this option for real), I look forward to something like this for enterprise users. Today, in my day job we deliver bunch of generative AI solutions for our customers. However, each time we make a project, it has its own API inference resource that needs setting up (e.g. Azure Foundry instance per environment). Of course the costs and token economics is a regular motif in design decisions. Since we build from scratch usually, each time we need to do additional plumbing to track usage across users. Having something similar could dynamically change the AI portfolio inside one company:

- One could save on duplicated spending by getting rid of several inference instances to just work with one per-seat expense of AI provider.
- User login can replace API keys even at app level (if custom login providers is supported and not only the ChatGPT account flow).
- User-based token tracking becomes unified by design.
- Make projects much more maintainable.

Quite surprising if we ever get this in this context, as providers do love to double dip as much as they want. I do hope that M$ thinks about it for their O365 suite for this. The EntraID flow for this could be a killer app. In the meanwhile, we are still limited to localhost project demos to play with the feature. Anything else currently requires a waitlist. Hope this becomes more accessible to anybody with their API account, and also get matched by competitors.
